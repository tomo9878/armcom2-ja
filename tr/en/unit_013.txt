MK VII Tetrarch	"The MK VII Tetrarch was a British tank designed to be light enough to be air-droppable. It entered production after the Battle of France, but it was not deployed until D-Day due to its inability to handle North Africa's climate. By then, it was ill-suited for frontline combat, and the very narrow tracks resulted in disappointing off-road mobility. All remaining tanks were withdrawn by Autumn 1944."
