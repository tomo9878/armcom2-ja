Yak-9	"Developed from the Yak-7, the Yakovlev Yak-9 was one of the best fighters of World War Two and continued to be used in the Korean War. It was introduced in late 1942. Yak-9B	"Developed from the Yak-7, the Yakovlev Yak-9 was one of the best fighters of World War Two and continued to be used in the Korean War. The Yak-9B was a fighter-bomber variant with an internal bomb bay behind the cockpit, carrying a bomb load of up to 400 kg. Because of its disappointing performance, only about 100 planes of this type were produced in early 1943."
Yak-9T	"Developed from the Yak-7, the Yakovlev Yak-9 was one of the best fighters of World War Two and continued to be used in the Korean War. The Yak-9T first saw action during the Battle of Kursk in July 1943."
Yak-9K	"Developed from the Yak-7, the Yakovlev Yak-9 was one of the best fighters of World War Two and continued to be used in the Korean War. The Yak-9K was a rare tank-buster variant, armed with a 45mm Nudelman-Suranov NS-45 cannon instead of the 20mm ShVAK cannon. Its powerful recoil made it extremely difficult to use the Yak-9K effectively, and only about 50 were produced in 1944."
