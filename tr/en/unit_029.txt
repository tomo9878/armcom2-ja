L3 Lf (Trailer)	"Developed from the British Mark VI tankette, the L3/33 was adopted by Italian forces in 1929. The Lf variant is armed with a flamethrower, and this sub-variant has an external fuel tank mounted on a trailer giving it more ammo capacity."
L3/38	"An upgrade to the L3/35 developed in 1938, a limited number of tanks were converted and saw service in 1943. The twin 8mm machine guns have been replaced by a single 13.2mm machine gun. The L3/38 also has torsion bar suspension which reduces friction and increases the tank's reliability."
L6/40	"First produced in 1939, the L6 was an attempt to develop a replacement for the L3 tankette. Although an effective armoured reconnaissance vehicle, it was already outclassed by the time of its introduction."
P26/40	"Designed in 1943, the P26/40 was the only Italian heavy tank deployed in World War Two. It was armed with an Ansaldo 75mm L/34 gun and two 8mm Breda 38 machine guns. Only very few P26/40 tanks were used by Italian troops. Most of them ended up being seized by the German forces after the Armistice of Cassibile."
R35 (ITA)	"Designed as a light infantry tank, the R35 is well-protected but its short 37mm main gun is slow to fire and lacks armour penetration power. Germany gave 124 captured R35s to Italy in 1942."
M11/39	"Designed to be the main medium battle tank of the Italian army, the M11 was under-armoured and its hull-mounted 37 anti-armour gun proved difficult to use effectively."
M13/40	"Developed from the M11, the M13 added a more powerful 47mm gun and a powered-traverse turret. From 1941 onward it became the standard medium tank in the Italian forces."
M14/41	"The M14/41 was a slightly improved version of the M13/40 with a more powerful diesel engine, a new lever for mud driving and a reworked wheel drive. It entered service in mid-1942."
Semovente da 47/32	"The Semoventes were Italian tank destroyers, designed on the model of the German StuG. Despite the disappointing performance of its main gun and its lack of any secondary armament, the Semovente da 47/32 was deployed on the Eastern Front, in North Africa, and in Italy."
Semovente da 75/18	"Introduced in January 1942, the Semovente da 75/18 was a dangerous threat to Allied tanks in North Africa. It was also often used in an improvised artillery role."
Semovente da 75/34	"Developed in 1942, the Semovente da 75/34 mounted a 75mm gun on the chassis of a M15/42 tank. Only 141 were built, and it only saw action in the defense of Rome in September 1943."
Semovente da 90/53	"The Semovente da 90/53 mounted a 90mm anti-aircraft gun on a modified M14/41 tank chassis. Only 30 were produced, owing to a shortage of guns. These units proved highly effective in North Africa."
Semovente da 105/25	"The Semovente da 105/25 mounts a 105mm gun on a modified M42 chassis, giving it a lower profile. Over 100 were built and some were deployed in the defense of Rome in 1943."
