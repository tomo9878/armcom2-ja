Entrenched/def	"Infantry and Gun units can be entrenched, which means they are protected by trenches, sandbags, and the like. Area fire and HE attacks against entrenched units will be less effective. Units lose entrenched status if they move, and cannot gain this status again during a battle."
Experience Points	"Experience Points"
Experience Points/def	"A measure of battle experience gained by your crew. At the end of each combat day, your VP score is awarded to each surviving crewman as Experience Points. Crewman who gain enough points to go up a level gain an Advance Point that can be spent on improving their stats or adding a new skill."
Fate Points/def	"Represents in an abstract way the forces of Fate protecting the player and their crew. Each Fate Point will automatically be used to save the Commander (if Player Commander is active) or can be used to force an incoming attack to automatically miss. A limited number of Fate Points are available each day."
Fatigue/def	"A representation of mental and physical fatigue experienced by crewmen. Each battle has the chance of adding fatigue points to crewmen, which decrease the effectiveness of their skills. Crewmen may lose Fatigue when waiting in place, or as the result of a random event. Crewmen lose all Fatigue Points at the end of each Combat Day."
Fighting Withdrawal/def	"Campaign day mission type. The enemy is mounting a strong attack against our lines. Destroy enemy units but withdraw into friendly territory if necessary."
Fire at Will/def	"Squad command allowing attacks when and wherever possible."
Fire Small Arms/def	"This command has the crewman fire a pistol or SMG (depending on whether they have the 'Spray and Pray' skill, at nearby targets, potentially applying 1-2 firepower on them."
First Aid/def	"A command open to all crewmen, if a fellow crewman has a Critical injury, this will give him a bonus to recover."
Flamethrower	"Flamethrower"
Flamethrower/def	"Also FTR. A weapon that projects burning fuel onto a target. Very effective against unprotected targets, less effective against armoured vehicles but can still penetrate through air vents and destroy them."
FTR/def	"See: Flamethrower."
Fortified/def	"Infantry and Gun units can be fortified, which means that they occupy a stone or concrete fortified position. Area fire and HE attacks against fortified units will be much less effective. Units lose fortified status if they move, and cannot gain this status again during a battle."
FP	"FP"
FP/def	"Firepower. An abstract representation of the effect of small-arms, machine gun, and explosive damage. Has the potential to reduce, rout, or destroy unarmoured units, and can injure any exposed crew in the player's armoured vehicle. Firepower is cumulative during the same turn, so FP applied from different attacks will be added up and resolved all at once at the end of a phase."
FT/def	"Fast Turret. A vehicle turret that can rotate very quickly, giving a small modifier to hit if a weapon on it attacks in the same turn as rotating."
FXT/def	"Fixed Turret. A vehicle with a superstructure that cannot rotate. Weapons are limited to firing toward the front of the vehicle."
G/def	"Gyrostabilizer. Helps to keep the turret stable while in motion, reducing the penalty for firing while moving. A Gunner needs to have the Basic Gyro Use skill to benefit from it, and the penalty is further reduced if the Gunner has the Advanced Gyro Use skill."
Grit/def	"Crewman stat, represents resistance to physical damage. Crewmen with high Grit will be less likely to be injured."
Gun/def	"A large-calibre weapon that fires a projectile. Note that in game terms, MGs are not counted as 'guns' for skills and stats purposes."
Gunner/def	"The crewman responsible for operating a Gun. Can sometimes be combined with another role, as with the Gunner/Loader position."
Half-Tracked/def	"Some vehicles have a set of tracks in the rear and a pair of wheels in the front to aid steering. These vehicles move like Wheeled vehicles but have a lower chance of getting Bogged Down."
Hamada/def	"Scenario terrain type. Flat hard-packed dirt covered in a layer of sharp stones. Units cannot try and Dig In here. HE effects enhanced since the stones act as shrapnel. "
Hatch	"Hatch"
Hatch/def	"A small opening near to a position on an armoured vehicle. Crewmen can usually operate their position with hatch open, giving them better visibility of the surrounding terrain, but leaving them vulnerable to incoming firepower. Some small turrets have the hatch on the rear of the turret, meaning that the main armament cannot be operated when the crewman is CE."
HD/def	"Hull Down. A vehicle that is Hull Down has its Hull protected behind something large and solid such as a hill or stone wall. Any incoming hits that strike the Hull will likely have no effect. HD status is centered on the direction shown, and is less effective one hexside to either side of that direction."
HE/def	"High Explosive. One type of gun ammunition, used against unarmoured targets. If a Hit is secured with HE, it will apply Firepower to the target. HE effectiveness can be affected by the weather, terrain, and target status (eg. fortified.)"
Heroic/def	"Sometimes enemy units may be inspired to become Heroic during a battle. Heroic enemies are less likely to surrender, less likely to move away from the player, and more likely to attack if they can."
Hex	"Hex"
Hex/def	"A six-sided shape, used in the game to represent an area of terrain. On the Campaign Day map, hexes are pointy-topped and represent an area of about 1.5 km across. In the Scenario Map, they are flat-topped and represent an area of about 120 meters across. The visual contents of a hex are abstract representations of varied terrain."
HMG/def	"Heavy Machine Gun. Usually operated by a team of 2-3 people with a large, tripod-mounted machine gun. Able to penetrate light armour and produces a fair amount of Firepower."
Hold Fire/def	"Squad command to not attack any enemy targets."
Hold the Line/def	"Campaign day mission type. Our position is under attack. Hold our ground at all costs."
Hull/def	"The body of a vehicle, usually better armoured than the Turret or Superstructure. The player tank's Hull is always facing upward on the Scenario map, and all other units rotate around it accordingly."
Hunker Down/def	"This command will have a crewman take cover as much as possible in a Crew Exposed position. This means they are more protected from the effects of incoming attacks."
HVSS/def	"Horizontal Volute Spring Suspension. An advanced type of suspension for tracked vehicles. Slightly improves base movement chance and slightly decreases bog chance."
Inferior Gun/def	"Some guns were known to be poorly designed or difficult to use effectively, and so always have a penalty to hit."
Jammed	"Jammed"
Jammed/def	"Weapons have a small chance of becoming jammed with each use. The crewman operating the weapon must try to un-jam the weapon during a subsequent turn."
KIA/def	"Killed in Action. The crewman has died during the Combat Day."
Knocked Out/def	"The armoured vehicle has been damaged to an extent where it can no longer operate, and the crew must Bail Out of the vehicle."
Knowledge/def	"Crew stat, applies a small bonus to all crew actions."
Landmine	"Landmine"
Landmine/def	"If landmines are present in a Campaign Day map zone and a battle takes place there, then every time any unit on the Scenario map moves or repositions, there's a chance that it will trigger a landmine explosion. This may destroy units or immobilize vehicles."
LMG/def	"Light Machine Gun. A portable weapon capable of a high rate of automatic fire. Riflemen and paratrooper squads may carry one of these weapons."
LoS	"LoS"
LoS/def	"Line of Sight. The Scenario Map is an abstract representation of a battlefield, with many details not shown. If two units have Line of Sight between them, it means that there is nothing blocking a potential attack or Spotting attempt. LoS is always reciprocal. To gain LoS on an enemy unit, either you or the other unit must move or reposition."
Loader/def	"The crewman responsible for loading and reloading a gun. With a Loader on a reload command, the Gun will have a higher chance of maintaining Rate of Fire."
